# The order mechanism, redrawn: ECM against one fixed group

Round 22. Same budget, one group per prime versus a fresh group per curve.

## 1. One ticket per prime, or one per curve

200 semiprimes `N = p q` with a 28-bit `p` and a 60-bit `q`. Pollard's `p - 1` gets `B1 = 2000`, `B2 = 100000`: it succeeds when the order of its base mod `p` -- a divisor of `p - 1` -- is smooth to those bounds, and a new base only moves among those divisors. Each ECM curve gets the same bounds and a fresh group order `p + 1 - t`.

| method, same B1 and B2 | succeeded | retries that succeed |
|---|---|---|
| Pollard p - 1 (one fixed group) | 109 of 200 | 0 of 91 failures, with four new bases |
| ECM, first curve | 122 of 200 | all, within 4 curves (median 1) |

| primes where | count | p - 1 succeeded | ECM's first curve succeeded |
|---|---|---|---|
| p - 1 is (B1, B2)-smooth | 110 | 109 of 110 | 66 of 110 |
| p - 1 is not | 90 | 0 of 90 | 56 of 90 |

ECM's first curve succeeds on 60% of the primes where `p - 1` is smooth and 62% of those where `p - 1` is hopeless: the curve's order has nothing to do with `p - 1`. Every group in the Pascal family -- the row mod `(N, f)`, the AKS fold, the norm-one torus, the q-deformation -- has order `p^d - 1` or a divisor, fixed by `p`; that is the whole reason they stalled.

## 2. The cost follows `p`, not `N`

| bits of p | bits of N | B1 | ECM curves (mean of 3) | ECM time (median) | Pollard rho (median) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 32 | 160 | 500 | 3 | 0.02 s | 0.11 s |
| 40 | 160 | 827 | 19 | 0.18 s | 1.80 s |
| 48 | 160 | 1917 | 12 | 0.22 s | -- |
| 56 | 160 | 4196 | 67 | 2.81 s | -- |
| 64 | 160 | 8772 | 113 | 7.59 s | -- |

With `N` fixed at 160 bits -- beyond the pure-Python quadratic sieve here -- both costs are set by `p`. Rho's grows as `sqrt p`, `2^4 = 16` times per 8 bits; ECM's grew 3.5 times per 8 bits between 40- and 64-bit `p`, the sub-exponential `L_p[1/2]` the order mechanism reaches once its groups can be redrawn -- Proposition 17's ceiling, attained.
